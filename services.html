Training Minus the slop

The fitness internet sells 30-day shreds, secret exercises, and coaches a language model made up this morning. You do not need any of that.

Show up. Add a little weight or a few reps over time. Form before ego. Walk a lot. Sleep. Take the rest day.

It is boring. That is how you can tell it works.

Nutrition Minus the slop

The food aisle has its own slop. Ultra-processed products are the feed in edible form: built for the sale, not the eater.

Mostly real food. Protein and plants at most meals. Water. Cook a little. No diets, no miracle foods, no shame.

I am more interested in why you eat at 11pm than in what you ate.

Emotional Well-Being The actual work
